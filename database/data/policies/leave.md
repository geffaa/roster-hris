# Leave policy

Synthetic demo policy for Nusantara Kreatif. It mirrors the leave rules configured in Roster.

## Annual leave
Every permanent and fixed-term employee earns 12 working days of annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave becomes available after 12 months of service. People who join during the year receive a prorated allowance in their first eligible year. Weekends and public holidays inside a leave request are not counted.

## Requesting leave
Request leave in the Roster app under Time off at least five working days before the first day, except for emergencies. Your manager approves the request. If your manager is on leave that day, the request goes to their manager. People without a manager are approved by HR. Half days (morning or afternoon) are allowed for annual and sick leave on single-day requests.

## Carry-over and balance
Unused annual leave does not carry over into the next year unless HR agrees in writing before 31 December. You can see your balance, used days and pending days in the app at any time. Pending requests are already deducted from the balance you see.

## Sick leave
Sick leave has no fixed yearly limit. Tell your manager before your shift starts. For absences longer than two working days, upload a doctor's note to Roster within three days of returning.

## Marriage, maternity and bereavement
Marriage leave is 3 working days per marriage. Maternity leave is 65 working days, approved by your manager and then HR. Compassionate leave for the death of a close family member is 2 working days.

## Unpaid leave
Unpaid leave needs approval from your manager and then HR. Unpaid days are deducted from base pay: monthly base salary divided by the working days in that month, for each unpaid day. Contributions to BPJS are not reduced.

## Cancelling leave
You can cancel a pending or future request in the app. If approved leave has already started, ending it early returns the remaining days after today to your balance.
