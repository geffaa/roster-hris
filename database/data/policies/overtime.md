# Overtime

Synthetic demo policy for Nusantara Kreatif, following Indonesian overtime rules (Kepmenakertrans 102/2004 and PP 35/2021).

## When overtime applies
Overtime is work beyond your shift that your manager asked for or agreed to in advance. Request it in the app under My attendance with the date, start and end time, and what the work was. Requests can be made up to 30 days ahead and must be between 30 minutes and 12 hours.

## How overtime is paid
The hourly rate is one 173rd of your monthly wage (base salary plus fixed allowances). On a working day, the first hour is paid at 1.5 times the hourly rate and every hour after at 2 times. On a rest day or public holiday in a five-day week, hours one to eight are paid at 2 times, the ninth hour at 3 times, and the tenth hour onwards at 4 times.

## When it is paid
Approved overtime is paid with the salary of the month in which it was worked. Overtime approved after the payroll for that month is locked is paid in the next month.
