# Attendance and working hours

Synthetic demo policy for Nusantara Kreatif.

## Working hours
The office pattern is Monday to Friday, 08:30 to 17:30, including a one-hour lunch break. Some teams work other patterns, such as release support from Tuesday to Saturday or a night support shift from 22:00 to 06:00. Your own pattern is shown on the Today screen of the app.

## Clocking in and out
Clock in and out in the Roster app. Clock-in opens two hours before your shift and closes when your shift ends. The time always comes from the server, not from your phone. At Jakarta HQ you must be within 120 metres of the office; Bandung Studio uses 100 metres. Clocking in does not work offline, so nothing is stored on your phone.

## Lateness
There is a 10-minute grace period. Arriving after the start time plus the grace period counts as late, and the minutes are shown on your attendance record. Repeated lateness is discussed with your manager; it does not reduce pay.

## Forgotten clock-outs
If you forget to clock out, the day is marked as having no clock-out. Send a correction from My attendance with your actual times and a short reason. Your manager approves it, and the original times stay in the record history.

## Remote work
Employees attached to the Remote location may clock in from anywhere. Office-based employees can work remotely up to two days a month with their manager's agreement; ask your manager to note it in Roster.

## Public holidays
Nobody is expected to clock in on public holidays. If you work on a public holiday, your manager logs it as overtime.
