# Payroll and payslips

Synthetic demo policy for Nusantara Kreatif.

## Pay day
Salaries are paid on the 25th of each month. When the 25th falls on a weekend, salaries are paid on the working day before. Payroll is calculated in the last week of the month and needs two approvals before it is locked and paid.

## Payslips
Your payslip appears in the Roster app under Pay once payroll is sent, and you can download it as a PDF. The payslip shows each earning, every deduction with how it was calculated, and the contributions the company pays on top of your salary.

## Tax (PPh 21)
Income tax is withheld every month using the government's effective rates (TER) for your tax status. In December, or in your last month if you leave during the year, tax for the whole year is recalculated. If more tax was withheld earlier than the year requires, the difference is returned to you in that month's pay.

## BPJS contributions
You contribute 2% of your wage to JHT, 1% to Jaminan Pensiun (up to the government's wage cap), and 1% to BPJS Kesehatan (up to a wage of Rp12,000,000). The company pays its own share on top, including JKK and JKM. Your contributions are shown on every payslip.

## THR
The religious holiday allowance (THR) is paid with the salary before Idul Fitri. After 12 months of service you receive one month of wage (base salary plus fixed allowances). With at least one month but less than 12 months, THR is prorated: months of service divided by 12, times your monthly wage.

## Bank account changes
Change the account your salary is paid into from Me in the app. HR approves every change before it is used, to protect your salary. Until HR approves, payroll keeps paying the current account.

## Questions about pay
If something on your payslip looks wrong, contact the payroll team before the 5th of the following month. Corrections are made in the next payroll run, never by changing a locked month.
